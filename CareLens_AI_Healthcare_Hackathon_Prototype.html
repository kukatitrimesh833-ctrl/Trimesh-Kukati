Understand your health report in simple language.

CareLens AI turns difficult medical terminology and report values into easy-to-understand information, creates a doctor-question list, highlights values that may need attention, and provides general health guidance.

What makes our solution different?

👤 Personalized: explanation based on age and user context.

🧠 AI-ready: LLM integration point + local fallback engine.

📋 Actionable: summary + questions for the doctor.

🔐 Privacy-first: prototype stores data only in this browser.

⚠️ Safety: This prototype provides educational information, not a diagnosis or prescription. A doctor or qualified healthcare professional should make clinical decisions. If symptoms are severe or life-threatening, seek emergency medical care.

How the prototype works

① Input

User pastes report text or uploads a TXT/CSV report.

② Understand

Term detection, value extraction and rule-based safety flags simulate an AI/LLM layer.

③ Explain

Plain-language summary, discussion points and doctor questions are generated.

Medical Term Translator

Type a medical term and get a simple explanation. This is an educational glossary, not a diagnostic engine.

Example: Type hemoglobin.

Questions to Ask Your Doctor

These questions are generated from the report findings and help the patient prepare for a consultation.

Analyze a report first to generate personalized discussion questions.

💧 Daily basics

Maintain hydration, regular sleep, balanced meals, and appropriate physical activity unless your clinician has given different advice.

🥗 Food awareness

Prefer a balanced plate with vegetables, whole grains, protein sources and appropriate portions. Dietary needs vary by condition.

🩺 Follow-up

Keep reports together, note symptoms and medicines, and take a list of questions to your healthcare professional.

🚨 When not to wait

If someone has severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, loss of consciousness, sudden weakness, severe bleeding, or another potentially life-threatening emergency, seek emergency medical care immediately rather than relying on this app.

Local Analysis History

The prototype saves only analysis summaries in this browser using LocalStorage. No server is used.
